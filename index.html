Made for small hands

Every button is a picture, so children who can't read yet can paint on their own.

12 real brushes

🌈

Every colour

80 ready colours, any colour you like, and rainbow paint that works with every brush.

🦕

Hundreds of pictures

Animals, dinosaurs, vehicles, sea creatures, food, toys and more to colour in.

⭐

Shapes, stamps & letters

Stars, hearts, octagons, letters and numbers — drag to any size.

😀

Emojis

Paint with emojis or stick big ones anywhere on the picture.

📷

Your own photos

Add a photo and cut out just the person — done on the iPad itself.

🏞️

Backgrounds

Castles, beaches, farms, jungles and more to paint on.

🖼️

Why it exists

Painting for Annabelle started as a dad making a drawing app for his five-year-old daughter — simple enough for her to use on her own, and safe enough to hand over without worrying. It's free so that other families can enjoy it too.
